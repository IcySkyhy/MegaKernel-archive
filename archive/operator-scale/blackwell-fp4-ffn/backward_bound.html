fp4 residual-double-GEMM: the training-step throughput wall

RTX 5070 Ti (sm120), 3090 MHz lock, S=8192, dense-equiv TFLOPS. Measured this session (bwd_probe.cu + train_flip_24.cu).

Step throughput / layer — three honesty tiers

Each scenario at GEMM-only (pure tensor rate) → +amortized compress → +activation requant (deployable). The requant tax is real and does not overlap (measured 3% hidden). Transposable-2:4 is the accuracy-viable path; +structured dW needs block masks (see right).

Accuracy cost of the 2:4 mask (flip task, relPJ ↓)

relPJ = ‖P−J‖/√S after 3000 steps fp4 QAT. Finer masks = better accuracy. Only block-2:4 (≥64) unlocks the dW 2× — and it collapses the flip (b 0.97→0.42). The accuracy-viable masks (per-row/transposable) leave dW dense.
The wall: the two dense dW gradient GEMMs (711 TFLOPS, the dense-fp4 ceiling) are ~40% of the step and cap the accuracy-viable (transposable) deployable step at ~840 < 900. The only route to >900 is a sparse dW, which needs block-2:4 masks that break accuracy on this permutation task. A genuine accuracy↔throughput tension at the 900 line.
